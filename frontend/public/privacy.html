Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Free Subtitle Generator is built so that your video never has to leave your device. This page explains, plainly, what that means in practice.

Your video and audio

We do not collect, upload, or store the video or audio files you process. Transcription, editing, styling, and export all run locally in your browser tab using your device's own processing power. We have no server that receives your files, and no record of what you process is kept anywhere by us.

The speech-recognition model

The first time you transcribe a video, your browser downloads an open-source speech recognition model (Whisper) directly from Hugging Face's servers, and caches it locally so it doesn't need to be downloaded again. That download request is made by your browser directly to Hugging Face, not through us, so Hugging Face's own privacy practices apply to that specific request. No part of your video or audio is included in that request.

Accounts and cookies

There is no account system. We do not use cookies, and we do not run any tracking or advertising scripts at this time. If that changes in the future, this page will be updated first, and any tracking will be disclosed here.

Standard server logs

Like effectively any website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information about requests to this site, such as IP address, browser type, and request time, for security and reliability purposes. This is infrastructure-level logging, not something we access or use for tracking individuals.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
